PRIMARY

Mining — this is the game

Everything in STRATUM starts with mining. Walk up to a resource node — a tree, an ore seam, an herb patch, a crystal formation — and left-click it. That's it. Every hit gives you a raw material, some gold, and (usually) a small amount of pending STRATUM.

What you're mining

NodeYieldsNotes
TreewoodCommon everywhere there's green ground
Ore seamoreDenser near rocky/volcanic terrain
Herb patchherbNeeded for armor and a few tools
CrystalcrystalRarest, gates the best gear tier

Most nodes take a couple of hits to fully deplete — you'll see it change state as you work it. A depleted node isn't gone forever: the planet regrows it on its own after a while. Nothing here is a quarry you can empty out — it's a world you settle.

Why your tool tier is everything

A bare-handed swing gets you the least of everything: least yield per hit, slowest pace. Every tool tier you unlock — flint → copper → iron → steel — mines more per swing, and never less than what you had before. This is the real progression curve in STRATUM: better tools aren't a side quest, they're the whole economic engine. See Crafting below for exactly how to get them.

The one thing to remember: if your income feels slow, the answer is almost always "craft the next tool tier," not "find a better spot."
SECONDARY — MULTIPLIES MINING

Crafting

Crafting doesn't compete with mining — it feeds it. Press C to open the craft panel. Every recipe shows exactly what it costs and what tool tier unlocks it; a recipe you can't afford or haven't unlocked yet is dimmed, not hidden, so you always know what's coming next.

Tool upgrades — do these first

The top card in the craft panel is always your next tool tier. Paying its cost raises your tool tier permanently and immediately increases how much every future swing yields. There's no reason to delay one you can afford.

Gear — weapons and armor

Below tools, recipes turn raw materials into weapons (more attack) and armor (less damage taken) across four tiers matching the tool ladder. Better gear makes fighting safer and faster, which matters once you're relying on monster kills for extra income too.

Batch crafting — craft more in one click

Holding Shift while clicking a recipe crafts as many as you can currently afford in a single request, instead of clicking the same card over and over. Same total cost, same total output, just less clicking.

Salvage — undo a craft you regret

The SALVAGE MODE toggle at the top of the craft panel lists everything crafted you're currently holding. Clicking one breaks it back down for roughly half its original materials back — not a full refund, but enough that trying a different gear path never feels like a dead loss.

TL;DR
Mine → unlock the next tool tier → mine faster/more → repeat. Gear and decor are there when you want them, but the tool ladder is what actually multiplies your income.
FOUNDATION

Claiming land

Left-click open, unclaimed ground to stake it — it costs a small amount of whatever material matches that terrain (dirt, grass, stone, and so on all cost something different). Once it's yours, it's yours permanently. There is no decay, no upkeep, and nobody — ever — can take a claim from you. Right-click your own claimed land to release it back to the frontier if you no longer want it; that's the only way a claim ever changes hands.

Claiming land isn't required to mine or fight, but structures, shops, and parcel deeds all need your own claimed ground under them.

ECONOMY

Gold & STRATUM — two currencies, two jobs

Almost everything that earns you something pays in both at once:

GOLD
Soft, in-game only. Spend it freely on shop listings and parcel deeds — there's no reason to hoard it.
STRATUM
The hard currency — STRATUM's real token, meant to move on-chain (Solana). Every action that grants it adds to your pending balance first.

Mining, crafting, and fighting all pay a small amount of pending STRATUM per action. There's no cap on how much you can accrue by playing.

SINKS

Spending STRATUM in-game

STRATUM isn't only something you claim off-world — you can spend pending STRATUM directly, right now, seven ways. Every spend burns most of what you pay and feeds the treasury — and the treasury funds everyone's GLDX:

Earth Requisition — SHIP TO EARTH

The SHIP TO EARTH button sends some or all of your pending STRATUM toward the colony's overall mission. A shipment of 50 or more STRATUM at once announces itself to every other player on the map — a real, visible milestone.

Structure Rush

Own an idle structure? Shift-click it to instantly finish whatever it's still producing, for STRATUM, instead of waiting out the timer. A plain click still just collects whatever has already finished.

Vanity wardrobe — [K]

Three cosmetics — Gilded Band (100), Ember Cloak (250), Starlight Scarf (500) — are bought with pending STRATUM, never earned. Zero stats, all signal. Buying unlocks; wear from the wardrobe like anything else.

Will refill

Out of will mid-session? REFILL WILL under the will bar restores it to full for 10 STRATUM. Only when you're actually below full — it refuses a full bar.

Shop listings

Every new shop listing costs 5 STRATUM pending, straight to the treasury. Sales still take the usual 2.5% cut on top — listing is the price of shelf space.

Settler tithes

Talk to Sable, Dray, or Ilo and begin a tithe: 50,000 STRATUM once, all to the treasury, for a permanent +10% reward yield while it holds. Upkeep is 100 STRATUM a week from pending (mostly burned); miss a week and the tithe lapses — cancel free anytime. The serious holder's subscription.

STRATUM vault

Stake pending STRATUM with STAKE ALL: 1% entry fee to the treasury, then your share earns a cut of a fixed weekly GLDX emission, pro-rata. UNSTAKE ALL returns it minus 2.5%. The pool's size (TVL) is public — deeper pool, steadier yield.

Land upkeep + dividends

Every week each owned tile burns 2 STRATUM from your pending (capped at 2,000 — land is never seized, short weeks just burn what exists). In return the treasury pays landholders 1 STRATUM per tile from fee income. Hold land, get paid; hold more, burn more.

Most of whatever you spend this way is permanently destroyed, not recycled — that's deliberate. It's what keeps STRATUM meaning something instead of only ever going up.

RISK & REWARD

Combat

Creatures roam every map with real AI — they notice you, chase, and hit back. Left-click one in range to strike. Kills grant XP, gold, and STRATUM, and your level climbs as you fight. Better gear (see Crafting) makes this safer and faster.

Dying has a real cost: a death drops half your carried raw materials right where you fell. Walk back to recover them before someone else does, or before they expire.
PASSIVE INCOME

Idle structures

Press I to see what you can build on your own claimed land. A structure quietly produces a resource over time even while you're off doing something else — check back later, or Structure Rush it (see Spending STRATUM) to finish it instantly.

This keeps earning whether you're logged in or not, and whether or not you've connected a wallet — a wallet only matters when you're ready to claim or convert what you've earned, never for whether you earn it. It's deliberately slower than playing actively: mining, crafting and fighting in person still pays far faster per minute than any structure ever will. Idle structures are a background trickle for when you're away, not a replacement for showing up.

PLAYER ECONOMY

Shops, deeds & trade

  • Shops — list something from your own claimed tile for a fixed price in another resource. A small treasury fee applies to real sales; gifting through direct trade never does.
  • Parcel deeds — mint a deed for a piece of your land and sell it to another colonist outright, fee included.
  • Direct trade — offer an item to a specific player, no fee, no escrow games; either side can cancel before it's accepted.
GETTING STRATUM OFF-WORLD

Wallet & claiming

You can play, mine, and earn gold and pending STRATUM with no wallet. Connect a Solana wallet from the HUD when you want to cash out. One wallet is one colonist.

  • Claim sends your pending STRATUM to that wallet on Solana. A fee stays with the treasury; you receive the rest. Until the treasury is funded and settlement is fully turned on, a claim is recorded and your pending balance is left untouched — nothing is ever lost.
  • Convert swaps gold for pending STRATUM, or pending STRATUM for gold — this one already runs entirely in the game ledger, no wallet or on-chain step required for the swap itself.

STRATUM is a real SPL token on Solana (Token-2022, with a 1% transfer fee that's the token's own on-chain config, not something STRATUM adds). Official contract: EtCLoVVQ87RfiJELMvcHxf1JwcSP2iNAaL73uacPFaLU — always verify this against the HUD's own CA display before trusting any other source.

Honest status: the mint is real and live. On-chain payouts still need a funded treasury and settlement fully turned on — until then every claim is queued, not lost. Convert already runs today.

THE FINE PRINT — ON PURPOSE, NOT HIDDEN

A few honest notes

  • STRATUM has no admins and no moderation queue, by design. Ownership of land is absolute and enforced by the server, not a person.
  • There is no way yet to buy gold or STRATUM with real money. Everything is earned by playing — mining, crafting, and fighting.
  • On-chain settlement (turning pending STRATUM into a real on-chain payout) is built but not yet switched on — see Wallet & claiming.
  • Real-money play-to-earn is a genuinely regulated space in most places. Play, earn, and enjoy — just don't treat anything here as financial advice or a guaranteed payout until settlement is actually live.
The land never resets. The provisions always do.
