STRATUM

One finite world per map. Each 1,048,576 tiles. They never expand.

The land is permanent. What you place, you own — nobody can ever overwrite it.
The wilderness is a commons. Anyone may build on unclaimed terrain.
The provisions reset. Ore, timber, herbs and beasts come back on their own timers.
You can only give land back — you can never take it from another.
