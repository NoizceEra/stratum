STRATUM

Earth's gold is gone. You're one of the first colonists sent to find more — one finite world per map, 1,048,576 tiles each, never expanding. Whatever you build here is what Stratum becomes.

Your claim is permanent. What you place, you own — nobody can ever overwrite it.
The frontier is unclaimed. Anyone may build on unstaked ground.
The planet provides. Ore, timber, herb and beast come back on their own — Stratum isn't a quarry you empty, it's a world you settle.
You can only release a claim — you can never take one from another colonist.
Refine what you mine. A kiln turns ore into more gold — the reason Earth sent you.
Play first. Mining and crafting earn gold and STRM with no wallet.
Wallet when you cash out. Connect a Solana wallet to claim STRM or convert gold. A fee is taken only on claim and convert.
